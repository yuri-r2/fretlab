    How Fretlab trains you

    • Short, daily sessions. A daily goal and streak keep practice consistent. Set your goal in settings.
    • Mixed practice blocks. Routines switch skills every few minutes instead of drilling one thing for an hour. In a study of advanced musicians, interleaved practice beat blocked practice on later performance (Carter and Grahn, 2016).
    • Drills that find your weak spots. The fretboard and ear trainers ask more often about the notes you miss or answer slowly, like spaced repetition flashcards.
    • Real numbers. One-minute changes (as taught by JustinGuitar) and the tempo log show measurable progress week to week.
    • Ears in context. Scale-degree training plays a cadence first, then the note, then resolves it home. It's the approach popularized by the Functional Ear Trainer app.
